Slide 7 appendix · Why Fabric Match?

The working that chose Fabric Match over the other two options.

Three screens, in the order they were applied: how each option compares against the unmet need, how they score on RICE, and the decision criteria that actually settled it. RICE is the screen; the criteria are the decision.

Solution comparison

Fabric in Motion Fabric Feedback Fabric Match
How directly does it solve the unmet need? Indirectly. A better guess from the same channel that already failed. Partly. Structured beats prose, but a number with no referent is the "180 GSM" problem crowdsourced. Directly. Supplies the referent — the only construction that turns a fabric fact into a decision.
How difficult is it to execute? Low. A studio protocol change. Moderate. A week of engineering; the hard part is getting buyers to fill sliders. High — but 60–70% of the rails already exist under size-and-fit.
How unique is it? Not unique. Video galleries are standard; several brands already shoot fabric close-ups. Fairly. Structured reviews exist; nobody has scoped them to tactile properties. No fashion marketplace has done it.
Can a competitor copy it? Within a quarter. Anyone with a studio. Easily. Anyone with reviews. Structurally hard. The moat isn't the feature — it's what you kept. Ajio cannot see your Myntra wardrobe, and the reference set compounds with every purchase.
